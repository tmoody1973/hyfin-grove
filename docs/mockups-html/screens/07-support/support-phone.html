Who we are

Accountable to you, our community.

HYFIN is a community-funded platform, part of non-profit Radio Milwaukee. Because we're community-funded, we're not beholden to corporate interests or traditional funders.

01 Culture

Showcasing the full spectrum of Black music, art, and cultural expression, from jazz to Amapiano, gospel to Afrobeats.

02 Tech & entrepreneurship

Creating opportunities for Black businesses and professionals to thrive and get recognition.

03 Youth & education

Investing in the next generation through Grace Weber's Music Lab and mentorship programs.

04 Storytelling

Amplifying Black voices, narratives, and experiences in Milwaukee and beyond.
